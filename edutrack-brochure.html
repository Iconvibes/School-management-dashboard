About EduTrack
What is EduTrack?
EduTrack is an all-in-one school management platform designed specifically for Nigerian schools. It replaces scattered spreadsheets, paper records, and manual processes with a single, connected system that every stakeholder can access — from the school admin to teachers, students, and parents.

Features
Everything Your School Needs
Built by people who understand Nigerian schools. Every feature is designed to solve real problems — from Naira-based fee tracking to WAEC-format report cards.
📋
Student Management
Enroll students, manage class arms, track academic progress, and maintain complete student records with photo IDs.
💰
Fee Collection
Create fee structures per term, track payments, send reminders, generate receipts, and manage outstanding balances in Naira.
📝
Grading & Reports
Record CA scores and exam marks, auto-calculate grades, generate professional report cards in PDF format.
📅
Timetable
Build conflict-free timetables with our smart scheduler. Assign teachers to arms and subjects with automatic clash detection.
📊
Attendance
Mark daily attendance per class arm, track trends, and send absence alerts to parents automatically.
🔔
Notifications
Push notifications, email alerts, SMS reminders — keep parents informed about fees, events, and their child's progress.
📱
PWA Offline Access
Works offline on phones and tablets. Teachers can mark attendance and grades without internet, syncing when reconnected.
🔒
Role-Based Security
Every user sees only what they need. Admins, teachers, students, and parents each have their own secure, tailored dashboard.
📚
Resources & Notes
Teachers share class notes, schemes of work, and study materials. Students access everything in one organized place.
