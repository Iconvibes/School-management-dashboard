## Post 6 · Grading matrix *(attach Slide 6 — Grading Matrix)*

For teachers: the days of calculators and look-up tables are over.

EduTrack's grading matrix is built for how teachers actually work:

✎ **Type scores, watch grades appear** — enter CA (out of 40) and Exam (out of 60); totals and letter grades (A–F) compute live as you type.

✓ **Per class arm, per subject** — SS1 Science, SS1 Arts, JSS… each stream keeps its own matrix and roster. Teachers only ever see their own classes.

⚙ **Batch save with safety** — unsaved changes get flagged, and one click saves the whole class. Scores feed straight into report cards and rankings.

★ **Best students, auto-ranked** — top performers by average surface automatically. Perfect for prizes, assembly and records.

The teacher's job is to teach. The platform should handle the arithmetic.
