The problem

notebooks & 2-week report cards

Report cards take weeks

Handwritten or Excel report cards mean a term-end crunch — teachers burn out and parents wait weeks for results.

Fees live in notebooks

Balances tracked in jotter books and spreadsheets — collections leak and nobody knows who truly hasn't paid.

Attendance is guesswork

No single record of who's in class day to day — and nothing connects attendance to results or reports.

Parents are in the dark

Parents only hear about results, attendance and fees when they ask — or when it's too late.

The result: a painful term-end crunch, uncertain cash flow, and no single dashboard where the school owner can see everything.
